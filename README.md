## 📌 Project Overview & Business Problem

Retail operations require seamless, cross-departmental visibility to balance revenue growth with healthy operating margins. **Panda Market** operates across diverse product verticals (Electronics, Home Appliances, Clothing, and Books) in multiple international territories.

### Key Business Questions Solved:
* **Executive Visibility:** What are our total sales, cost burdens, and bottom-line profit margins?
* **Product Profitability:** Which subcategories and products act as growth drivers vs. low-margin volume fillers?
* **Customer Behavior:** How do purchasing patterns differ across demographics, gender segments, and quarters?
* **Geographical Expansion:** Which regions, countries, and metropolitan cities drive the highest revenue and profit?
